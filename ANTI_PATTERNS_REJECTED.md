# Anti-Patterns Rejected

- Share-card gradients were rejected because the architect brief locks the card to a solid background and a single accent.
- Motivational quotes on the share card were rejected because they turn a measurement product into generic fitness fluff.
- Bright red "bad session" treatment was rejected because the brief explicitly protects the post-session moment from shame.
- In-session telemetry stacks were rejected because the athlete is 2m away and cannot parse a dashboard mid-rep.
- Cheerleading voice cues were rejected because this product behaves like a calm coach, not a class instructor.
- Queueing every cue was rejected because silence is part of the coaching system and overlapping speech destroys comprehension.
- Generic camera toasts were rejected because "step back" and "move phone lower" are more actionable and more sport-specific.
- Blocking session start until perfect setup was rejected because the brief explicitly preserves an override path.
- Email-first onboarding was rejected because first-session value must come before account creation.
- Tutorial carousels were rejected because they lengthen time-to-first-score without teaching anything the product can’t demonstrate live.
